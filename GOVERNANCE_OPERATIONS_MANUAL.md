## 🚨 INCIDENT RESPONSE

### Incident Classification
```
P1 (Critical): Service down or data compromised
├─ Response time: 15 minutes
├─ Escalation: Immediate
└─ Communication: Every 30 minutes

P2 (High): Significant impact but workaround available
├─ Response time: 1 hour
├─ Escalation: Within 2 hours
└─ Communication: Hourly

P3 (Medium): Limited impact or degradation
├─ Response time: 4 hours
├─ Escalation: Next business day
└─ Communication: Every 4 hours

P4 (Low): Minor issues or cosmetic problems
├─ Response time: 24 hours
├─ Escalation: Weekly review
└─ Communication: As needed
```

### Response Team
```
Incident Commander
├─ Tech Lead (identifies root cause)
├─ Communications Lead (updates stakeholders)
├─ Product Lead (decision-making)
└─ Customer Support (handles inquiries)
```
